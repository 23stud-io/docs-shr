The Whisper Rewards System distributes $SHROOMY tokens weekly based on DeFi activity and daily check-ins.

## How It Works

1. **Activity Tracking**: System monitors lending, staking, locking, and airdrop participation
2. **Points Accumulation**: Earn points from positions active in the last 7 days
3. **Daily Check-ins**: Collect points and NFT tickets once per day
4. **NFT Tickets**: Receive Bronze, Silver, or Gold tickets based on activity and VRF probability
5. **Weekly Distribution**: Automatic $SHROOMY distribution every Monday

## Supported Activities

- **Lending**: Supply and borrow operations
- **Staking**: SHROOMY token staking
- **Token Locking**: Lock tokens for various durations
- **Airdrops**: Participate in airdrop activities

Activity is tracked using a 7-day rolling average. Only positions active within this period contribute to reward calculations.

## Daily Check-ins

- **Frequency**: Once per 24 hours
- **Requirements**: Maintain minimum activity across protocols
- **SHROOMY Holder Bonus**: +5% chance for Gold/Silver tickets

## Weekly Rewards

Every Monday, $SHROOMY tokens are automatically distributed based on tickets:

| Ticket Type | Pool Share |
|-------------|------------|
| Gold | 50% |
| Silver | 30% |
| Bronze | 20% |

Rewards are sent directly to wallets - no claiming required.

## Getting Started

1. Start DeFi activities (lending, staking, locking)
2. Check-in daily to collect points and tickets
3. Receive automatic weekly $SHROOMY rewards

## FAQ

**Q: Can't check-in today?**
A: Need minimum activity level across protocols.

**Q: When do points update?**
A: Daily, based on 7-day rolling average.

**Q: When are rewards distributed?**
A: Every Monday automatically.
